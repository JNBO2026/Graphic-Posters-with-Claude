How the visual development is shown

Shown with day 06's real process: two character takes, the 3D model that couldn't be downloaded, the magenta cut-out, the Kling turntable, and the first composite where the rider hid the word. Every day I'd log each step as I go, with a verdict: kept, cut or fixed. It sits just before the follow slide, goes into the LinkedIn PDF too, and adds one 2.5 second scene to the Reel, so the Reel becomes 12.5 seconds, still on the same beat.
