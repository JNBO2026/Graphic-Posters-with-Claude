What shows when you hover a poster

Desktop only. Four levels, from a small title card to nothing at all. Hover the posters inside each frame.

ATitle card
A small paper card rises from the bottom: the day and the title. Nothing else.
BPrompt snippet
The first dozen words of the prompt as a pull quote, with how many words were typed in the 60 seconds. The prompt is the hook.
CLedger card
The title, then three ruled rows: prompt length in words, made with (code only or the model), and the date posted. Matches the Departures tiles.
DCursor only
Nothing over the poster at all. The blame opal cursor carries the day and the title. The poster stays clean.
