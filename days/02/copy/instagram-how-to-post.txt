Feed (carousel)
1. New post, select all five in Feed-carousel/ in order: 1 to 5 (1080 × 1440, the tallest the feed shows). 1 is the poster, 5 asks for the follow and the save.
2. Paste caption.txt.
3. Make sure the site link is in your bio. The caption says "link in bio".
4. Saves and shares count for the most, so answer comments fast in the first hour.

Story
1. Post Story/story.png.
2. Add the stickers in stickers.txt: the link sticker first, then the poll.
3. Post it within an hour of the feed post, so people who tap through land on something new.

Dark versions are in dark-version/ if you’d rather.
