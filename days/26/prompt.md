Geometry and the body, after Oskar Schlemmer's Triadic Ballet at the Bauhaus: a dancer is a cone, a sphere, a spiral, a disc, and moving is just shapes changing places. Two figures built only from primitives, on a bare stage, lit hard from one side so they throw long shadows. They turn and step in a slow loop, the way a mechanical ballet would. Real 3D, real soft shadows, flat Bauhaus colour.
