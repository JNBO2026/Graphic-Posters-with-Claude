Light and colour as physics, not as swatches. A beam of white light enters a glass prism and comes out as a spectrum, traced properly: Snell's law at every face, a different refractive index for every wavelength (Cauchy), the colours of each wavelength worked out from how the eye sees them. Dark room, real dispersion, the fan of colour as wide as the page. The prism slowly turns and the spectrum sweeps.
