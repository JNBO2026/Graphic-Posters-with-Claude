Colour field, after Rothko. No type, no image, no idea except colour and the edge between two colours. Two vast soft-edged fields on a ground, built the way he built them, out of thin glazes laid one over another, the edges feathered by a dry brush, the weave of the canvas showing through. And slowly breathing: the fields swell and settle, the glazes shift, the way the paintings seem to move if you stand in front of them long enough.
