1. New post. Upload post-image.png (1080 × 1350, portrait takes up the most feed space).
2. Paste post-copy.txt. Check the first two lines show before "see more".
3. Post it, then straight away add first-comment.txt as the first comment. Links in the post itself get less reach, links in a comment don't.
4. Reply to every comment in the first hour. That’s when LinkedIn decides how far it goes.
