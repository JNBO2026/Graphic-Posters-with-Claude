Reel (the one for reach)
1. New Reel, upload Reel/reel.mp4 (always light, about 27 seconds, 1080 × 1920). Every change in it lands on a 120 BPM beat, so pick one of the songs from the handover and line the start of the track up with the first frame.
2. Paste caption.txt. Pick the poster moment (around 1 second in) as the cover.
3. Turn on "Also share to feed".

Carousel (the one for saves)
1. New post, select every image in Feed-carousel/ in number order (1080 × 1440). 1 is the poster, the contact sheet and the before and afters show how it got there, the last asks for the follow and the save.
2. Paste caption.txt. Make sure the site link is in your bio. The caption says "link in bio".

Story
Share the carousel to your Story, then add the stickers in story-stickers.txt: the link sticker first, then the poll.

Saves and shares count for the most, so answer comments fast in the first hour.

The carousel is in one theme, light on even days, dark on odd days, so the grid keeps its checkerboard. The Reel is always light.
