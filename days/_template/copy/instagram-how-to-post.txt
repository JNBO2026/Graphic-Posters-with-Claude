Reel (the one for reach)
1. New Reel, upload Reel/reel.mp4 (10 seconds, 1080 × 1920). Every change in it lands on a 120 BPM beat, so pick one of the songs from the handover and line the start of the track up with the first frame.
2. Paste caption.txt. Pick the poster moment (around 1 second in) as the cover.
3. Turn on "Also share to feed".

Carousel (the one for saves)
1. New post, select all five in Feed-carousel/ in order: 1 to 5 (1080 × 1440). 1 is the poster, 5 asks for the follow and the save.
2. Paste caption.txt. Make sure the site link is in your bio. The caption says "link in bio".

Story
Share the carousel to your Story, then add the stickers in story-stickers.txt: the link sticker first, then the poll.

Saves and shares count for the most, so answer comments fast in the first hour. Dark versions are in dark-version/ if you’d rather.
